# DeFi Accountancy Website Specification

## 1. Project Overview

### Company
**DeFi Accountancy**

### Business Type
Accounting, tax preparation, bookkeeping, and business services firm.

### Core Services
- Personal tax return preparation
- Business tax return preparation
- General tax services
- Bookkeeping
- Sales tax services
- Business licensing
- Corporate and LLC formation services
- Related accounting and business compliance services

### Current Business Context
DeFi Accountancy currently serves approximately:
- **80 tax preparation clients**
- **A small number of recurring bookkeeping clients**

The website should support the current tax practice while helping the company grow its recurring bookkeeping and business-services revenue.

---

# 2. Website Purpose

The website will serve primarily as a **professional marketing, credibility, and lead-generation website**.

It should make DeFi Accountancy appear established, trustworthy, professional, and easy to work with.

The website should help accomplish the following:

1. Establish credibility for prospective and existing clients.
2. Clearly explain all services offered by DeFi Accountancy.
3. Give referrals a professional place to learn about the company.
4. Generate inquiries from new prospective clients.
5. Make it easy for visitors to call, email, or request a consultation.
6. Educate existing tax clients about additional services.
7. Generate more recurring bookkeeping clients.
8. Generate business tax, sales tax, formation, and licensing clients.
9. Build a foundation for future organic search traffic.
10. Support future expansion of the accounting practice.

---

# 3. Primary Business Objective

The website should help reposition DeFi Accountancy from being perceived primarily as a tax-return provider into a:

> **Full-service tax, accounting, bookkeeping, and business services firm.**

The business should particularly use the website to increase the number of clients using multiple services.

Example customer lifecycle:

**Business Formation**

→ Bookkeeping

→ Sales Tax Services

→ Business Tax Preparation

→ Personal Tax Preparation

→ Ongoing Tax and Accounting Support

The website should encourage this type of long-term client relationship.

# 39. Security Requirements

Because this is an accounting business, security should be treated as a core requirement.

Minimum requirements:

- HTTPS everywhere
- Valid TLS certificate
- Secure hosting
- Server/software dependency updates
- Secure form handling
- Input validation
- Spam protection
- Rate limiting where appropriate
- No sensitive data stored unnecessarily
- Environment variables for secrets
- No secrets committed to source control
- Secure administrative accounts
- Multi-factor authentication on critical services where supported
- Backups where appropriate
- Least-privilege access
- Security headers
- Dependency vulnerability monitoring

---

# 40. Privacy Requirements

The site must include a privacy policy explaining, where applicable:

- What data is collected

- Contact form data

- How users can contact the business about their information

# 41. Accessibility Requirements

- Semantic HTML

- Accessible navigation

# 58. Definition of Success for Version 1

Version 1 is successful when:

1. A referral can search DeFi Accountancy and find a professional business presence.
2. A visitor understands the company's services within seconds.
3. A visitor can easily determine how to contact the company.
4. A potential client can submit a structured inquiry.
5. Existing tax clients can discover additional services.
6. Bookkeeping is positioned as a core offering.
7. The website establishes trust without unnecessary complexity.
8. The website is fast, responsive, accessible, and secure.
9. The site provides a strong foundation for SEO and future marketing.
10. The site can evolve without requiring a complete rebuild.
